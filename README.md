# Indi Gupte

Indi Gupte: brand, partnerships and research at RiskMandate, a team of AI agents behind him, and looking for work experience.

Every page under `src/` is generated by `node scripts/generate.mjs` (and `scripts/designs.mjs`) from `data/facts.mjs`, the master copy of the facts, each with a source in `data/sources.md`; never edit a generated page by hand. `/behind-the-scenes/` is written at build time by `scripts/behind.mjs`. The team and its policies are in `team/`. Nothing is loaded from other hosts. Pages: home, What I built (`/built/`), How I work (`/how-i-work/`), Languages (`/languages/`, with a figure drawn in SVG), Background (`/background/`), Looking for (`/looking-for/`) and Use with AI (`/ai/`, with a plain text `/llms.txt`). Facts come from Indi's LinkedIn profile, his own WhatsApp post and the public records linked on each page, all read 2026-11-18. Contact is by LinkedIn only.
