### Conflicts, and how the site handles them

- **Commits.** 277 (newsroom, 3 to 13 November) against 309 (the sum of the per-agent counts on his dashboard, derived by the researcher, a longer window: his conductor's last commit is on 16 November). The site uses 277 with its source and window, and explains 309 in a note.
- **Agent count.** 27 agents, 23 running, on his dashboard and the team register; "23 agents running" on the team's home page. These agree. A 28th, his Lisbon Tech Fringe conductor, is not in the 27 and is listed apart.
- **Team size.** The newsroom says "six of us"; the team's site says "Seven people". Not about Indi; the site gives no team size.
- **His role.** "The message, outreach, and the factory of examples" (team site), "communities and organisers" (newsroom), "editor" (PrompTugal.news). Different parts of the work; the site gives each with its source.
- **Pronoun.** One line of his brief page uses "her"; the factory brief and the newsroom use "he". The site uses "he".
- **Business cards.** His post says "I designed our business cards"; the team's site says his designer agent drew the rounds, he chose his card on 4 November, and Design A was chosen for printing on 5 November. The site quotes his post, then gives the record, each with its source.
- **University.** His summary says he will read Modern Languages and International Relations from next autumn; his education lists University of Oxford, Modern Languages, Italian and Portuguese (2027). The site shows both as he lists them, without reconciling them.
- **Strangers in the Loop release date.** Not printed; 14 November is derived from "released the next day" after 13 November, and the site marks it as derived.
