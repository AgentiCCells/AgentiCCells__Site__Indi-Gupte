## Conflicts

- **Commits.** The newsroom gives 277 commits by his agents on the team's site (counted for 3 to 13 November); the per-agent counts on his dashboard sum to 309, and his conductor's last commit is dated 16 November. The two are likely different windows; use 277 with its source and window.
- **Agent count.** 27 agents, 23 running, on both his dashboard and the team register; the home page gives "23 agents running". These agree. A 28th agent, conductor.fringe@PrompTugal.indi, appears on Lisbon Tech Fringe and is not in the 27.
- **Team size.** The newsroom says "six of us"; the team's site says "Seven people" and his brief "one of the seven". Not about Indi directly.
- **His role.** The team's site says "The message, outreach, and the factory of examples"; the newsroom says "communities and organisers"; PrompTugal.news says "editor". They describe different parts of the work, not a contradiction.
- **Pronoun.** One line of the brief page uses "her" ("every agent of hers"); the factory brief and the newsroom use "he" and "his". Follow the factory brief.
- **Business cards.** His post says "I designed our business cards"; the team's site credits his agent designer@web-summit.indi with the designs, and him with choosing them.
