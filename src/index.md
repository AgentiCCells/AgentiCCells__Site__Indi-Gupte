## What he built

### RiskMandate: Agent Behaviour Policies

RiskMandate writes an Agent Behaviour Policy for every AI agent a company runs: what the agent can reach, what the company authorised it to do, the gap between the two, and what stands in the way. Indi joined RiskMandate in November 2026 as Brand and Partnerships Lead, and at Web Summit Lisbon his part of the team's work was the message, outreach and the factory of examples.

His part: Brand and Partnerships Lead, from November 2026. Check: https://riskmandate.ai

Sources: Indi Gupte's post to a UK Web Summit WhatsApp group; [RiskMandate](https://riskmandate.ai/index.md); [Indi Gupte's LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/indi-gupte-26960b318); [Indi Gupte's role card, Web Summit team site](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/team/indi/index.md)

### PrompTugal.news

PrompTugal.news sets out to map the universe of AI in Portugal: the companies, the people, the events and how they connect, every fact with its source and the date it was read. Its articles are written by AI agents and edited by people. Indi is one of its three editors, with Dinis Cruz and another team member.

His part: Editor. Check: https://promptugal.news

Sources: Indi Gupte's post to a UK Web Summit WhatsApp group; [PrompTugal.news, front page](https://promptugal.news/index.md); [PrompTugal.news, about](https://promptugal.news/about/index.md); [PrompTugal.news, the board](https://promptugal.news/about/board/index.md)

Each person on the team ran one conductor agent; Indi's lane was communities, and his conductor made the communities page: who runs the week's events, and how to join.

## How he works with AI agents

A team of 27 AI agents, 23 running. 277 commits by his agents on the team's site, 3 to 13 November 2026.

- His agents draft; he sends every message.
- His social agent drafts one post a day for RiskMandate's LinkedIn page; Indi posts.
- His infographic agent tests each page on a stranger (a small sub-agent) before Indi sees it.
- His synthetic-users agent tests what he works on with invented personas, starting with the business cards.
- His accountant agent measures the tokens his agents use and proposes changes, so each agent gives its best output for the fewest tokens.
- His prompter agent improves his prompts so that his tasks get done in one or two prompts.
