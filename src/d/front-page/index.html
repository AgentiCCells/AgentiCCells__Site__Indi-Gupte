RiskMandate: Agent Behaviour Policies

RiskMandate writes an Agent Behaviour Policy for every AI agent a company runs: what the agent can reach, what the company authorised it to do, the gap between the two, and what stands in the way. Indi joined RiskMandate in November 2026 as Brand and Partnerships Lead, and at Web Summit Lisbon his part of the team's work was the message, outreach and the factory of examples.11823

His part: Brand and Partnerships Lead, from November 2026. riskmandate.ai

PrompTugal.news

PrompTugal.news sets out to map the universe of AI in Portugal: the companies, the people, the events and how they connect, every fact with its source and the date it was read. Its articles are written by AI agents and edited by people. Indi is one of its three editors, with Dinis Cruz and another team member.1151617

His part: Editor. promptugal.news

Lisbon Tech Fringe

On 10 November at 19:09, as Storm Charles reached Lisbon, Web Summit called off its second day. On Wednesday 11 November the team built Lisbon Tech Fringe, an independent guide to the side events around Web Summit Lisbon: it went live at 12:57, and the Fringe app was out for testing at 21:11. Each person on the team ran one conductor agent; Indi's lane was communities, and his conductor made the communities page: who runs the week's events, and how to join.1192021221214

His part: The communities lane and the communities page. lisbontechfringe.com

Strangers in the Loop

Strangers in the Loop is RiskMandate's second song, produced by Indi. Dinis Cruz writes: "I helped a little with the lyrics, but it is mainly his." It was released the day after the first song, which came out on 13 November.1324

His part: Producer. Listen on Spotify

The business cards

Indi's designer agent drew the RiskMandate business cards for Web Summit in two rounds, and his synthetic-users agent tested them on invented personas. On 4 November he chose his card: the real seal, the brand's accent strip, no event lines. On 5 November Dinis Cruz chose Design A for printing: three audience cards (Executives, GRC and Security), 85 x 55 mm, about 200 of each.11094

His part: Designer, working with his designer agent. The cards on the team's site

The factory of company pages

On 3 November, at Indi's go, his developer agent opened the factory: one infographic page for each company marked "meet" on the team's company map. Each page shows what the company does as a flow, the risks it faces from AI agents as a mind map, and how RiskMandate helps. It is the "factory of examples" in his team role, and the company research he names in what he brings.54

His part: Set it going and steered it; his developer and factory agents built the pages. His brief on the team's site
