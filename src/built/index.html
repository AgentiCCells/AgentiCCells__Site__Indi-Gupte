RiskMandate: Agent Behaviour Policies

In his words: "a record of what each AI agent can reach, what it's allowed to do, and the gap between the two."1

RiskMandate writes an Agent Behaviour Policy for every AI agent a company runs: what the agent can reach, what the company authorised it to do, the gap between the two, and what stands in the way. Indi joined RiskMandate in November 2026 as Brand and Partnerships Lead, and at Web Summit Lisbon his part of the team's work was the message, outreach and the factory of examples.1823

His part: Brand and Partnerships Lead, from November 2026.

PrompTugal.news

In his words: "a newsroom mapping AI in Portugal, run by people and AI agents together."1

PrompTugal.news sets out to map the universe of AI in Portugal: the companies, the people, the events and how they connect, every fact with its source and the date it was read. Its articles are written by AI agents and edited by people. Indi is one of its three editors, with Dinis Cruz and another team member.151617

His part: Editor.

Lisbon Tech Fringe

In his words: "when Storm Charles cancelled Wednesday, we built a guide to the side events, and an app, the same day."1

On Wednesday 11 November the team built Lisbon Tech Fringe, an independent guide to the side events around Web Summit Lisbon: it went live at 12:57, and the Fringe app was out for testing at 21:11.
