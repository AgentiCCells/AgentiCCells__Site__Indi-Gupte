## The loop (one round)

```
 brief ─▶ research ─▶ design ─▶ review of design ─▶ copy ─▶ build ─▶ release ─▶ live review ─▶ report
 (conductor) (researcher) (designer) (reviewer)       (writer) (developer) (conductor) (reviewer)   (conductor)
                                                         ▲                         │
                                                         └──── a failure goes back ┘
```

1. **Brief.** The conductor writes the round's aim at the top of `team/rounds/N.md` and takes "before" screenshots of the live site (`node scripts/shots.mjs https://indi-gupte.agenticcells.site team/rounds/img/rN-before`).
2. **Research.** The researcher reads the public record the brief names and writes `data/research/RECORD.md`.
3. **Design.** The designer writes `team/rounds/N/design/PROPOSAL.md`.
4. **Review of the design.** The reviewer gives a verdict per change in `team/rounds/N/review/DESIGN.md`. Rejected changes drop out; "pass with changes" carry the reviewer's conditions.
5. **Copy.** The writer writes the words and facts the passed changes need, with sources.
6. **Build.** The developer builds the passed changes, runs the gates locally and commits. It does not push.
7. **Release.** The conductor reads the diff, then runs `scripts/release.sh` (build, validate, leak scan, check-commits, role-scope, then rebase and push to `dev`, which deploys; CI tags v0.1.N).
8. **Live review.** The reviewer checks the live site at 390 and 1366 px in `team/rounds/N/review/LIVE.md`. A failure goes back to step 5 or 6 in the same round.
9. **Round report.** The conductor completes `team/rounds/N.md`: what changed, before and after screenshots, the verdict, what is open, what Dinis or Indi must decide; then one line to cells@AgentiCCells.dinis.
